Cyboard Privacy Policy
1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains what information the Cyboard mobile application processes and why. The application is developed by Cyboard. Cyboard is a tool that allows you to monitor riding information such as speed, battery status, and voice notifications by connecting to your electric skateboard.

2. Information Collection and Use
The Cyboard application does not collect, store, or share any personal data (such as name, email address, phone number, etc.) directly from its users. The technical permissions required for the application to function are as follows:

Bluetooth Access: Required to communicate with your skateboard and receive real-time speed and battery data.

Location Access: Due to the technical requirements of Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) technology, the operating system may request location permission to scan for and connect to devices. Cyboard does not track, record, or send your actual location to any servers.

Voice Notifications: The application provides voice alerts for speed data to enhance riding safety. This process occurs entirely on the device.

3. Third-Party Services
The application does not use any third-party data analytics tools or advertising networks to perform its core functions. Your data remains strictly between your device and your skateboard.

4. Security
Since we do not collect user data, no personal information is stored on our servers. The security of the connection between your device and your skateboard is protected by standard Bluetooth protocols.

5. Children’s Privacy
Since our application does not collect any personal data, it does not engage in any data processing activities that would violate the privacy of children. However, parental supervision is recommended for safe riding.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. In the event of any changes, we will notify you by posting the new policy on this page.
